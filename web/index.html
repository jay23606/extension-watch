Extension Watch

Browser extension risk, monitored

Your team's Chrome extensions, reviewed before they turn against you.

An extension that was harmless when installed can gain "read all your data on all websites" in a silent update. Extension Watch downloads each package, reads its manifest, scores the risk, and alerts you the moment that changes.

Methodology

Every score is a sum of transparent rules. No black box, no magic number.

Each permission is worth points proportional to how much it can reach. The risk score is the total, capped at 100. Grade bands: 0–19 A, 20–39 B, 40–59 C, 60–79 D, 80+ F.
